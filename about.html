Imperial Solution Company

01

Company overview

Imperial Solution Company (ISC) is a MISA-licensed limited liability company based in Dammam, Eastern Province, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The company operates across manufacturing development, specialist construction solutions and industrial supply, and is in the process of establishing local manufacturing capability in the Eastern Province.

As a newly established entity, ISC builds credibility through its model, partnerships and capability — not through a fabricated project history.

02

Our position

ISC is positioned as a Saudi localisation platform: a company that identifies established manufacturers abroad, partners with them, and transfers proven technology into local production within the Kingdom — so that the Kingdom can be supplied with Saudi-made products rather than imports over time.

ISC is not positioned as a trading intermediary or a general contractor. The model aligns industrial ambition with precise statements of current stage: establishing, planned and in development where production has not yet commenced.

03

Vision

To develop local industrial capability in Saudi Arabia by bringing proven international manufacturing technologies into the Kingdom through structured partnership and long-term localisation.

04

Mission

To build a credible Saudi platform for industrial localisation and supply — combining partnership, technology transfer, engineering discipline, quality assurance and industrial materials sourcing for buyers and manufacturers alike.

  • Partnership with established specialist manufacturers
  • Technology transfer into the Kingdom
  • Specialist engineering support
  • Quality-led sourcing and documentation
  • Industrial supply for Saudi project and plant environments
