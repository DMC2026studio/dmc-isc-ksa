Technology

Glassfibre reinforced systems

GRC

Glassfibre reinforced concrete

Cementitious composite panels and elements for architectural façades and decorative / structural cladding applications.

GRP

Glassfibre reinforced plastic

Fibre-reinforced polymer products for construction and industrial environments where corrosion resistance and formability are required.

GRG

Glassfibre reinforced gypsum

Lightweight gypsum composites for interior architectural features and specialised finishing applications.
